I am a PhD student in Molecular Biology at the University of Geneva, working in the Department of Molecular and Cellular Biology under the supervision of Prof. Paul Guichard and Dr. Virginie Hamel.

My research uses in situ structural biology to understand how cellular machines are built inside the cell. My PhD project focuses on the molecular architecture of the ciliary transition zone, the gate at the base of the cilium that controls which proteins enter and leave it. I combine cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET), cryo-FIB milling and lift-out of cells and tissues, and ultrastructure expansion microscopy (U-ExM) to link native 3D structure with molecular identity.
